# 03 · User guide

Organised by role and task. Menu names are shown in **bold**; the sidebar only shows what your role is allowed to use.

---

## Getting around

- **Sign in** with the username and password HR gave you. The first time, you must choose your own password (at least 8 characters with letters and numbers) before you can continue.
- **Sidebar:** sections for *My workspace*, *People*, *Time and leave*, *Compensation*, *Talent* and *Administration*. A marigold number is the count of items waiting for your decision.
- **Search:** press **Ctrl + K** (or `/`), or click the search box at the top, and type a page name, a person's name or an employee ID such as `ST-1004`. Use ↑ ↓ and Enter.
- **Light or dark:** the moon/sun button in the top bar switches theme. By default HR-MIS follows your Windows setting; once you choose, it remembers.
- **Your name (top right):** My profile, Change password, Sign out.
- **Colours mean something:** green is the main action and "done"; marigold means "needs attention" or "pending"; red means a problem or a permanent action such as Delete.
- **Tables:** use the search box and filters above each list; ✏️ edits, 🗑️ deletes; click a name to open the profile.
- **Exports:** every "⇩" button downloads a CSV that opens directly in Excel (Bangla and ৳ display correctly).
- **Printing:** payslips, ID cards and reports open a print window. Choose *Save as PDF* to keep a file.
- On a phone, open the menu with ☰.

## Employee (My Workspace)

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Record my arrival | **Dashboard → Check in now**, or **My Attendance → Check in**. Check out the same way before leaving |
| See my attendance | **My Attendance**; use ◀ ▶ to change month |
| Apply for leave | **My Leave → Apply for leave**. Pick the type and dates; the box shows how many days it counts and your balance. Tick *Half day* for half a day |
| Cancel leave | **My Leave → Cancel** (pending, or approved leave that hasn't started) |
| See / print payslips | **My Payslips → View / Print** (available once payroll is finalized) |
| Ask for a salary advance | **My Payslips → Request an advance / loan**. Payroll approves it; installments come off your salary automatically |
| Claim an expense | **My Claims → New claim**. You can edit or delete it while it is pending |
| Update my phone / address | **My Profile → Update my details** |

## Line manager

Everything an employee can do, plus the following, **for your direct reports only** (you can't see other teams' records):

- **Approve leave:** Dashboard → *Approvals*, or **Leave → Requests** (filter *Pending*). Approve, or reject with a reason.
- **Approve claims:** **Expense Claims** → Approve / Reject.
- **Team attendance:** **Attendance → Daily attendance** and **Monthly register** show only your direct reports.
- **Team reviews:** **Performance → Add review**. Score the five factors from 1 to 5, add goals and comments, then set the status to *Submitted*.
- You never see salaries, and you cannot approve your own leave or claims.

## HR officer / HR manager

### Daily
1. **Attendance → Daily attendance:** today's sheet. Employees who checked in themselves or were imported already show.
   - Click **✔ Mark unmarked as present** for the usual case, then correct the exceptions (absent, late, half day, WFH, on duty).
   - Click **💾 Save changes**. A check-in after *office start + grace* becomes **Late** automatically.
   - From a biometric device: **Attendance → Import from device**, then paste the CSV export or choose the file.
2. **Leave → Requests:** decide on pending requests. Approved leave appears as *On leave* in attendance.
3. Check the **Dashboard** approvals list and today's numbers.

### Hiring someone
1. **Recruitment → Job openings → Add job opening.**
2. Add candidates and move them along the **Candidate pipeline** with ◀ ▶.
3. When a candidate reaches *Hired*, click **→ Onboard**. The employee form opens prefilled. Complete the job and pay sections and save.
   - Administrators can tick **Create a login** to issue a username and a one-time password.
4. The new profile has an **Onboarding checklist**. Tick the items as they are done (appointment letter, NID, bank account, ID card, IT accounts, laptop, induction, PF).
5. Print the **🪪 ID card** from the profile.

### Changing someone's job or salary
Open the profile → **🔄 Promote / transfer / exit** and choose:

| Event | Fields | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Promotion | new designation, grade, optional new gross | designation, grade, salary |
| Transfer | new unit / department / manager | placement and reporting line |
| Increment | % or new gross | gross salary |
| Confirmation | — | employment type → Permanent, confirmation date |
| Resignation / Termination / Retirement | last working day | status (On Notice until that date), exit date and an **offboarding checklist**. The day after the last working day the status becomes final and the person's login is switched off automatically |
| Re-hire | — | status Active, new joining date |

Every event is kept under **Career history**. **Lifecycle → Confirmations due** lists probationers whose six months are ending.

### Documents
Profile → **Documents → Upload document** (NID, certificates, contracts and similar, up to 10 MB). Employees can download their own documents from My Profile.

### Leave administration
- **Leave → Leave types & policy:** change entitlements, add types (e.g. Study, Hajj), mark types as unpaid or calendar-day.
- **Leave → Opening balances / carry forward:** add +/− days per employee per year (e.g. carried-forward earned leave or encashment).
- **Leave → Requests → Apply on behalf** for staff without a login. Tick *Approve immediately* if the leave is already agreed.
- **Leave → Balances:** remaining / entitled for everyone; export to CSV.

### Other regular tasks
- **Holidays:** add next year's holidays every December. Confirm lunar dates with the government gazette.
- **Notice Board → Add notice:** shown on every dashboard until the expiry date.
- **Training:** add a program, then **👥 Participants** to nominate staff and tick who attended.
- **Assets:** **→ Issue** to an employee; **↩ Return** when they leave (part of the offboarding checklist).
- **Lifecycle → Disciplinary:** record warnings and show-cause letters, with the outcome.

## Payroll officer — monthly procedure

| Step | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Make sure attendance and leave for the month are complete | Attendance → Monthly register | Absent, half-day, unpaid leave and repeated lateness reduce pay |
| 2. Approve pending loan requests | Loans & Advances | Installments start from the "first deduction month" |
| 3. Open the month | **Payroll**, then pick the month with ◀ ▶ | Shows a *preview*; nothing is saved yet |
| 4. Generate | ⚙️ **Generate payroll** (tick *Festival bonus* in Eid months) | Creates the draft |
| 5. Adjust | Type **OT hrs**, **Additions** (arrears, allowances) and **Other ded.** directly in the table | Each change recalculates that row |
| 6. Review | Totals cards and the register; open any **Payslip** | **↻ Recalculate** after late attendance corrections |
| 7. Finalize | 🔒 **Finalize** | Locks the figures, records loan repayments, and releases payslips to employees |
| 8. Pay the bank | ⇩ **Bank advice** (account-wise CSV for the bank) | Also ⇩ **PF statement** for the PF trust and ⇩ **Register** for accounts |
| 9. Close | ✔ **Mark as paid** | |
| Mistake after finalizing? | **Reopen** (only before *paid*), fix, then finalize again | Loan repayments are reversed automatically |

Salary rules and every percentage are explained in [09-payroll-and-policy-rules.md](09-payroll-and-policy-rules.md).

## Administrator

- **Users & Roles → New user:** username, role, linked employee (needed for self-service and manager access) and a temporary password.
- **Edit** a user to change the role, re-link or disable the account. Disabling signs them out everywhere.
- **Reset password** sets a temporary password and forces a change at next sign-in.
- **Roles & permissions** tab: the full permission matrix.
- **Audit Trail:** filter by record type, action and date; 🔍 shows exactly which fields changed.
- **Settings:** company name and ID prefix, office hours, weekend days, late rule, probation length and payroll percentages. Then click **💾 Save settings**.
- **Settings → Download backup** at least weekly; see [10-operations.md](10-operations.md).
